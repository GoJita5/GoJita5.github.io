This section is for short write-ups of things I am learning, mostly mathematics. Writing a topic down is how I find out whether I understood it.

Inline math is written \(f : X \to Y\) and display math looks like this:

$$ \chi(\mathcal{L}) = \deg(\mathcal{L}) + 1 - g $$

That is the Riemann–Roch formula for a line bundle \(\mathcal{L}\) on a smooth curve of genus \(g\) (the version with the correction term \(h^1\) is more precise). I plan to write about it when I get there.
