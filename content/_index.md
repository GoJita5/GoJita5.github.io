I study fundamental mathematics in the International Mathematics Master at the National Higher School of Mathematics. This year's courses include elliptic curves, Lorentzian geometry, dynamical systems and several complex variables, and my thesis is in algebraic geometry.

I like making things work. I have built regression and gradient-boosting models on real datasets, and I write small tools for my own use. I am heading toward software engineering, with a particular interest in application security.

This site holds my projects, the talks I have given, and notes on what I am learning.
