A team project from NHSM for the Regression Analysis course. We built a predictive model for diabetes risk from lifestyle data.

- Cleaned the data, then fitted multiple linear regression and logistic regression models.
- Compared the models with \(R^2\), ROC AUC and other performance indicators.
- Tools: pandas and scikit-learn.
