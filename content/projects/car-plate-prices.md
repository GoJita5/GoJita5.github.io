We built a model that predicts the price of a Russian car plate from the combination of letters and numbers on it and the time it was sold.

- Engineered around 70 features from only two raw inputs: the plate name and the time of sale.
- Trained several models, tuned their parameters and compared their performance.
- Tools: NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, random forest.
